About Prophet Edmond Ocran

Meet The Founder

Edmond Ocran is a young prophet raised by God at Pentecost University to awaken a generation that hears God clearly and speaks boldly. What God started in him in Sowutoum, He will use to shake nations.

Vision: Raise prophets, intercessors and leaders for this generation.

Mission: Train, equip and send young people who hear God clearly.

Base: Pentecost University Campus, Sowutoum, Accra, Ghana

Verse: Jeremiah 1:7 - Do not say, I am too young.
